# Myia Mack | Portfolio

## About
This is my personal portfolio website. It introduces who I am, what I'm learning
as an Intern Tech Consultant at i.c.stars, and the projects I've worked on.
Visitors can explore my journey through a game-style **Level Select** page,
where each level shows a different part of my growth.

**Live site:** https://myiamack.github.io/Portfolio/

## Why I Built It
My purpose word is **Disrupt**: seeing what's broken, saying what others won't,
and standing with people until it changes for the better. Tech was where things
first clicked for me, long before I knew it could be a career. I built this site
to show my technical skills to recruiters and the tech community, and to tell
the story of how my roots led me to tech.

## How to Use
- Use the top menu to jump to **About**, **Projects**, or **Contact**.
- In **Level Select**, click a tile to open that level:
  - **Roots** – Wellness
  - **Sprouting** – Technology
  - **Branching Out** – Leadership
  - **In Bloom** – Business
  - **Harvest** – Career
- At the top, **Featured: My Live Demo** opens my To Disrupt game.
- Hover over a Level Select tile to preview what's inside.
- Scroll to **Projects** to see my Medline, Geek Week SQL Survival Guide, Buffalo Soldiers comic, and Year of the Horse work.
