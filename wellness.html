Wellness: what keeps me grounded

Emotional: Reframing How I See Myself

The reframe that has helped me most is shifting my focus from "What will they think of me?" to "Does this help the team?" I lean on it when I feel hesitant to speak up in a meeting. Letting go of how others might perceive me has made me more confident and more present in the room.

Mental: Keeping My Mind Right During Geek Week

Geek Week was intense, so I protected my focus by breaking hard problems into analogies and patterns I already understood instead of letting them overwhelm me. Helping classmates who were still working through their tasks kept me grounded, because explaining something to someone else calmed me down and sharpened my own understanding.

Physical: Lessons From the Farm

Working on an organic farm at Growing Home, from planting seeds to harvesting crops, showed me how much being outside and moving my body resets my mind. Now that most of my day is spent in front of a screen, I make a point to step away, get outside, and move so I come back to my work refreshed.

Financial: Dos and Don'ts for My Past Self

Running House of Nova taught me lessons I wish I had known sooner. Do track every sale and expense, set clear goals, and price your work for the value it brings. Don't undervalue your time, and don't mix business and personal money. These habits turned my business from a passion into something I can plan and grow.
