Debugging: The Floating Tiles

While scrolling down my portfolio, I noticed my Level Select tiles were floating on top of the header instead of sliding under it. I learned the cause was the transform I used to make the tiles lift on hover, which puts them on their own layer above the header. The fix was one line of CSS: adding z-index: 10 to the header so it always stays on top. It taught me that every style choice can have side effects, so I need to be specific about how I want each part of my page to behave.

Where It Clicked: Task 5

Coding clicked for me during Geek Week, around Task 5, when we moved into Excel. I had used Excel in at a technology high school, and I realized the formulas followed the same patterns I learned in math. It felt like receiving downloads: my brain was doing a wide search of everything I had ever learned to make all this new information digestible in a short time. I also learned how I react under stress and how to push past it while staying focused. Going forward, I know I won't always understand everything at first, but if I push and encourage myself in a healthy way, I will succeed.
