Business: Turning problems into solutions

Client Project: Signal for Medline

As Project Manager and Scrum Lead for Canary Consultants, I helped lead Signal, an AI-assisted web app for Medline, a medical supplier with more than 45,000 employees and 70 distribution centers. Signal groups recurring warehouse incidents into trackable problems, so teams can see patterns instead of isolated events. I managed our sprint backlogs, wrote the executive summary recommending a four-capability MVP, and presented our recommendations to Medline stakeholders and i.c.stars mentors.

Consulting Company: Canary Consultants

Canary Consultants is the five-person consulting team I lead as Project Manager at i.c.stars. Like a canary in a coal mine, our name is about spotting problems early, and our motto, "Detect. Protect. Empower.", guides how we work: find the real issue, protect the people affected by it, and empower them with a solution they can use. Building a company identity together, with our own logo, team agreement, and run-of-show, gave five different working styles one shared direction.

Entrepreneurship: House of Nova

In 2021, I founded House of Nova, an independent jewelry brand, and I run every part of it: product design, sourcing, pricing, marketing, and customer engagement. I have built a base of more than 30 buyers, most of whom purchase multiple pieces per order, and I sharpened my strategy through two entrepreneurship programs: the Sunshine Enterprises Community Business Academy and the Evolve Entrepreneurial Hub at Olive-Harvey College. Running my own business taught me to think like an owner, which is exactly how I approach every client project.
