SQL Survival Guide: My mistakes, turned into lessons

Geek Week SQL Survival Guide

The hardest tasks for me were Task 5 and Task 14. I kept making mistakes with punctuation and recalling the right formulas for each task. I got through it by creating analogies and using pattern recognition from similar problems I had already solved. I wrote this guide for classmates who were having a hard time or didn't have support, and I wanted it to be passed down to the next cohort. It taught me that if you can't explain something in your own words to someone else, you might not fully understand it yet.
